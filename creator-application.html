✨ How Afrotious Nation Works ✨

Afrotious Nation is an independent, creator-powered marketplace and cosmic universe. Creators earn from their own products, games, and digital experiences — while Afrotious Nation earns a small marketplace fee that keeps the platform running.

Creator Earnings Model

  • Creators earn from their own sales, products, UGC items, and game experiences.
  • Afrotious Nation earns a small marketplace fee (typically 10%) from each sale.
  • This fee covers hosting, domain, development, marketing, and future expansion.
  • No monthly fees. No upfront costs. No pay-to-join.
  • You earn from your creations — Afrotious Nation earns only when you earn.

Why This Model Exists

Afrotious Nation is built by an independent creator with no external funding. The marketplace fee ensures the platform can sustain itself without charging creators or relying on ads.

What This Means for You

  • You keep the majority of your earnings.
  • You never pay out of pocket to participate.
  • You join a growing cosmic brand and marketplace ecosystem.
  • Your creations become part of Afrotious Nation’s universe — both online and in Roblox.
