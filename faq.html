Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about Afrotious Nation, the marketplace, creator roles, and earnings.

Afrotious Nation uses a creator-first earnings model. Creators earn from their own products, games, and digital experiences. Afrotious Nation earns a small marketplace fee that keeps the platform running.

How the fee works:

• Creator receives 90% of each sale

• Afrotious Nation receives 10%

• This fee covers hosting, domain, development, marketing, and future expansion

• No monthly fees, no subscriptions, no pay-to-join

Why this model?

Afrotious Nation is currently sustained through marketplace fees, allowing us to operate without relying on ads. In the future, optional ads or sponsorships may be introduced to support expansion — but never in a way that disrupts creators or the cosmic experience.

How Do Creators Join?

Creators apply through the Creator Application page. Roles include Roblox game creators, fashion designers, UGC creators, and handmade artists.

How Do Earnings Work?

Earnings are processed through Stripe. Creators receive payouts directly from their sales. Afrotious Nation only earns when creators earn.

What About Roblox Creators?

Roblox creators earn from their own games, UGC items, and brand integrations. Afrotious Nation earns from cosmic brand partnerships and marketplace bundles.
