Creator Earnings

Afrotious Nation is built to empower creators across handmade fashion, digital items, and game experiences. Earnings come directly from your own products and creations.

Earnings Breakdown:

• Creator keeps 90% of each sale • Afrotious Nation receives 10% • No monthly fees • No subscriptions • No pay-to-join

This model ensures the platform stays sustainable while keeping creators in control of their income and creative freedom. Afrotious Nation only earns when creators earn.
