Melanindian Waves — Radio Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Melanindian Waves — Nightscape Radio (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects information related to radio submissions and visits to our website.

2. Information We Collect

  • Submission Information: Artist name, email address, social links, genre, and track file (MP3/WAV).
  • Communication Data: Messages you send us, removal requests, or support inquiries.
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To review and process your radio submission.
  • To contact you about approval, placement, or questions regarding your track.
  • To feature your track on Melanindian Waves radio, playlists, and promotional content.
  • To maintain and improve the performance and security of our website and radio services.

4. Content & Broadcast Use

When you submit music, you grant us permission to store your submission details and broadcast your track on Melanindian Waves, including playlists, rotations, and TikTok Live sessions.

5. Cookies & Analytics

We may use basic cookies or analytics tools to understand traffic and improve the experience.

6. Data Sharing

We may use trusted third-party services for hosting, analytics, email delivery, or file storage. These providers only receive information necessary to perform their services.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect submission data and personal information.

8. Your Choices & Rights

  • You may request updates or corrections to your submission details.
  • You may request removal of your track from rotation.
  • You may ask us to delete your submission data where legally permitted.

9. Children’s Privacy

Melanindian Waves is not directed to children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests:
