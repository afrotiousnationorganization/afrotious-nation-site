Afrotious Nation – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Afrotious Nation collects, uses, and protects your information. By using this site, you agree to this policy.

2. Information We Collect

A) Information You Provide

We may collect information you provide directly, including: email submissions, creator applications, music submissions, UGC submissions, order information, and contact forms.

B) Automatically Collected

We may automatically collect IP address, browser type, device type, usage data, and cookies.

C) Third-Party Data

We use third-party services such as Stripe (payments), Netlify (hosting), Roblox (UGC integration), and TikTok, Spotify, SoundCloud (embeds). These platforms may collect data according to their own policies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate the marketplace, process payments, manage creator submissions, run Cosmic Radio (Melanindian Waves), integrate UGC into Roblox, improve the website, prevent fraud, and comply with legal requirements.

4. How We Protect Your Information

We use SSL encryption, secure hosting via Netlify, secure payments via Stripe, and limited access controls. We do not sell your data.

5. Sharing Your Information

We may share data with Stripe (payments), Netlify (hosting), Roblox (UGC integration), and TikTok, Spotify, SoundCloud (embeds). We do not share data with advertisers.

6. Cookies

We use cookies for site functionality, analytics, and user experience. You may disable cookies in your browser, but some features may not work properly.

7. Children’s Privacy

Users under 13 may not use the site. Users under 18 must have parental permission.

8. Data Retention

We retain data as long as necessary to operate the marketplace, comply with legal obligations, and maintain creator accounts.

9. Your Rights

You may request deletion, correction, or access to your data, or removal from creator programs by contacting: afrotiousnation@gmail.com

We may update this policy at any time. Continued use of the site means you accept the updated policy.
