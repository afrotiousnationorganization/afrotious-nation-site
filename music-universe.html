Afrotious Nation – Music Universe

Welcome to the Music Universe of Afrotious Nation — where handmade cosmic creations meet sound, story, and Melanindian Waves.

1. What Is the Music Universe?

The Music Universe is the sound dimension of Afrotious Nation. It connects:

  • Melanindian Waves (cosmic radio station)
  • Creator-submitted music
  • Fashion + music integration
  • Roblox experiences and UGC
  • TikTok Live and playlists

This page explains how music lives inside Afrotious Nation, how creators can participate, and how everything stays legally safe and clean for Roblox, TikTok, and streaming platforms.

2. Melanindian Waves – Cosmic Radio

Melanindian Waves is the official Afrotious Nation radio station. It exists as:

  • A cosmic radio stream concept for TikTok Live
  • A curated playlist experience (Spotify / SoundCloud)
  • A future in-game radio station inside Afrotious Nation City (Roblox)

Melanindian Waves focuses on:

  • Raw, bold, fierce, calm, and cosmic sounds
  • Non-explicit versions of tracks for Roblox-safe playback
  • Independent creators who keep their masters and rights

3. Creator Music – How It Works

Afrotious Nation is a platform, not a record label. Creators:

  • Keep 100% of their masters and royalties
  • Submit music for playlists, Melanindian Waves, and cosmic lore integration
  • Can be featured alongside Afrotious Nation’s own music and brand

Music can be used for:

  • Melanindian Waves radio streams
  • Cosmic ambient soundscapes
  • Fashion + music bundles
  • Roblox experiences (clean versions only)

4. Creator Music Terms (Simple Summary)

Full legal details are in the Terms & Conditions, but here is the simple version:

  • Creators must own the rights to the music they submit.
  • Creators must provide a non-explicit version for any Roblox or family-friendly use.
  • Afrotious Nation may feature, promote, and integrate submitted music across its platforms.
  • Afrotious Nation does not claim ownership of creator music.
  • Creators can request removal of their music at any time.

5. Music + Fashion + Cosmic Lore

Music in Afrotious Nation is tied to:

  • Handmade fashion pieces and accessories
  • Cosmic lore stories and character concepts
  • UGC items and Roblox experiences
  • Visual themes like purple, nebula, electric, and calm

The goal is to create a full sensory experience: what you wear, what you hear, and what world you step into all feel connected.

6. TikTok Live & Streaming

Melanindian Waves and the Music Universe can appear on:

  • TikTok Live (cosmic radio streams, creator showcases)
  • SoundCloud playlists and embeds
  • Spotify playlists (curated, not label-owned)

Streaming is used for exposure and discovery. The main focus is building the Afrotious Nation brand and giving creators a place to be seen and heard.

7. Future Roblox Integration

In the future, Melanindian Waves and the Music Universe may connect to:

  • Afrotious Nation City (Roblox experience)
  • In-game radio zones and listening rooms
  • Cosmic lore quests tied to music themes

All Roblox-related content will follow Roblox’s community guidelines and terms of use, with clean audio and compliant UGC.
