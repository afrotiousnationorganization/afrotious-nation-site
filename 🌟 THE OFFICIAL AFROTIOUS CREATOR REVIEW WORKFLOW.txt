🌟 THE OFFICIAL AFROTIOUS CREATOR REVIEW WORKFLOW
This is the full workflow YOU follow as the admin.

I’m giving you the exact order, the exact checks, and the exact actions.

⭐ 1. Check Netlify Identity → Users
This is where you confirm:

The user is verified

The user is invited

The user is approved

The user is not pending

The user is not unverified

If they are NOT verified → resend invite.  
If they ARE verified → continue.

This step confirms the creator is a real, authenticated user.

⭐ 2. Check Netlify Forms → Submissions
This is your review queue.

Every form you created appears here:

creator-application

brand-upload

music-upload

product-submission

role-application

ANY other form you added

Inside each form you will see:

Submission data

Uploaded files

Creator email

Timestamps

Role selection

Portfolio links

Brand assets

Music files

This is where you review EVERY submission.

⭐ 3. Review the submission content
For each submission, check:

✔ A. Does the creator meet the role requirements?
Portfolio quality

Brand quality

Music quality

Product quality

Correct category

Correct role

Correct submission type

✔ B. Are the files valid?
Images load

Audio plays

PDFs open

No corrupted files

✔ C. Is the creator legitimate?
No spam

No bots

No fake accounts

No stolen content

✔ D. Does the submission follow your guidelines?
No copyrighted music

No stolen brand assets

No inappropriate content

No low‑quality uploads

This is your quality control step.

⭐ 4. Approve or Deny the submission
You do this manually.

✔ If APPROVED:
Send your approval email (you already have this template).
Tell them:

They are approved

Their role is active

Their dashboard is unlocked

Their uploads are accepted

Their payout onboarding is next

✔ If DENIED:
Send your denial email (you already have this template).
Tell them:

They were not selected

They can reapply

They can improve their portfolio

They can submit again later

This is your decision step.

⭐ 5. Add their role in Netlify Identity (optional)
In Identity → Users:

Click the user

Add a role label (optional)

fashion-creator

music-creator

brand-creator

product-creator

general-creator

This is ONLY for your organization — it does NOT affect permissions.

⭐ 6. Send Stripe Connect onboarding (if payouts are needed)
If the creator will earn money:

Send them the Stripe Connect onboarding link

Stripe collects tax forms

Stripe handles payouts

Stripe handles compliance

You do NOT collect tax forms manually.

This is your payment onboarding step.

⭐ 7. Add their approved content to the website (if needed)
Depending on the role:

✔ Fashion creators → add their products
✔ Music creators → add their tracks
✔ Brand creators → add their brand page
✔ Product creators → add their listings
This is your content publishing step.

⭐ 8. Notify the creator their content is live
Send a simple email:

“Your brand is now live.”

“Your music is now published.”

“Your products are now visible.”

This is your final confirmation step.
