✨How Afrotious Nation Works✨

Afrotious Nation is an independent, creator-powered marketplace and cosmic universe. The platform is designed so creators can earn from their own products, games, and digital experiences — while Afrotious Nation earns a small marketplace fee that keeps the platform running. This allows us to stay sustainable, independent, and creator-first.

Creator Earnings Model

• Creators earn from their own sales, products, UGC items, and game experiences. • Afrotious Nation earns a small marketplace fee (typically 10%) from each sale. • This fee covers the cost of hosting, domain, development, marketing, and future expansion. • No monthly fees. No upfront costs. No pay-to-join. • You earn from your creations — Afrotious Nation earns only when you earn.

Why This Model Exists

Afrotious Nation is built by an independent creator with no external funding. The marketplace fee ensures the platform can sustain itself without charging creators or relying on ads. This keeps the experience clean, cosmic, and creator-focused.

What This Means for You

• You keep the majority of your earnings. • You never pay out of pocket to participate. • You join a growing cosmic brand and marketplace ecosystem. • Your creations become part of Afrotious Nation’s universe — both online and in Roblox.

🌌 Roblox Game Creator – Build a Game Inside Afrotious Nation City

About This Role

Afrotious Nation's City is expanding with creator‑built experiences. We are looking for Roblox developers who want to build their own game inside our city, accessible through a branded portal. Your game becomes part of the Afrotious Nation universe, connected to our fashion marketplace and cosmic brand identity.

Responsibilities

• Build a unique Roblox experience • Maintain • Collaborate on cosmic‑themed branding and UI • Optionally create matching UGC items or outfits to showcase in the game. • Work with Afrotious Nation to align your game’s theme with the marketplace

Requirements

• Experience with Roblox Studio • Ability to script in Lua • Ability to design environments or gameplay loops • Willingness to collaborate on brand integration • Optional: ability to create UGC items

What You’ll Build

• A playable Roblox game inside Afrotious Nation City • A portal entrance branded with your creator identity • Optional matching real‑life clothing + Roblox outfit sets • A creator profile page on AfrotiousNation.org

Add your portfolio, screenshots, and any Roblox experience you’ve built.
