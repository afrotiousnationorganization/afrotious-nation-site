Melanindian Waves — Nightscape Radio

Welcome to Melanindian Waves, the official nightscape radio division of Afrotious Nation. A cosmic broadcast sounding our thoughts and amplifying our dreams. Featuring creators, dreamers, musicians, and storytellers from across the Afrotious Universe.

What is Nightscape Radio?

Nightscape Radio is a neon‑lit, dreamwave broadcast blending cosmic ambience with creator‑submitted music. Every track is curated to match the Melanindian aesthetic — futuristic, soulful, and deeply imaginative.

How Creators Get Featured

Creators can submit clean versions of their music through the Music / Brand Submission Creator Application, found on the "Creator Application" page. Approved tracks are added to our playlists, featured on TikTok Live shows, and highlighted in weekly spotlights.

Music Selection Guidelines

• Clean version required • No copyrighted samples • Must match cosmic / nightscape theme • High‑quality audio • Must pass review

The Cosmic Theme

Melanindian Waves blends neon aesthetics, dreamwave ambience, and Afrofuturistic storytelling. Every broadcast is designed to feel like a journey through a glowing nightscape.
