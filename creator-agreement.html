Afrotious Nation Creator Agreement

Independent Contractor Terms & Platform Guidelines

This Creator Agreement outlines the terms, responsibilities, and payout structure for creators participating in the Afrotious Nation ecosystem. By continuing with onboarding, you acknowledge and accept the terms listed below.

1. Independent Contractor Status

Creators are classified as independent contractors, not employees of Afrotious Nation.

  • You manage your own taxes and financial reporting.
  • You are not eligible for employee benefits.
  • You control your own creative process and work schedule.
  • You may work with other brands, platforms, or clients.

2. Stripe Connect Onboarding

Afrotious Nation uses Stripe Connect to handle all creator payouts. Stripe manages:

  • Tax form collection (W‑9 for U.S. creators, W‑8BEN for international creators)
  • Identity verification
  • Bank account setup
  • Automatic payouts
  • 1099‑NEC generation for eligible U.S. creators

Afrotious Nation does not collect, store, or process your tax forms or banking information. All sensitive data is handled securely by Stripe.

3. Marketplace Earnings & Commission Structure

Creators earn revenue from sales, submissions, or brand placements across Afrotious Nation platforms.

The standard payout structure is:

  • Creator Earnings: 90%
  • Afrotious Nation Platform Fee: 10%

Stripe automatically splits payouts according to this structure.

4. Content Ownership & Licensing

Creators retain full ownership of their original work. By submitting content, you grant Afrotious Nation a non-exclusive license to:

  • Display your work on the Marketplace
  • Feature your brand or music in Afrotious Nation City
  • Use your assets for promotional or platform-related content

5. Removal or Termination

Creators may request removal of their content at any time. Afrotious Nation may also remove content that violates platform rules or legal guidelines.

6. Acceptance

By continuing with Stripe Connect onboarding, you confirm that you have read and agree to the terms outlined in this Creator Agreement.
