Radio FAQ

Answers to the most common questions about submitting your track to Melanindian Waves. If you need additional help, contact support or check the Radio Workflow page.

How long does review take?
Review typically takes 24–72 hours depending on submission volume. You will receive a confirmation once your track has been reviewed and placed.
Can I submit more than one track?
Monthly subscribers can submit unlimited tracks. One-time submitters can submit one track. Free trial users can submit unlimited tracks until the trial ends.
What genres do you accept?
We accept R&B, Hip-Hop, Electronic, Lo-Fi, Indie, Pop, Ambient, Experimental, and related subgenres. Tracks are placed based on vibe, not strict genre labels.
Where will my track be played?
Your track will be placed into one of our 7 nightly shows based on mood, energy, and style. See the Radio Schedule page for full show descriptions.
How do I get spotlighted?
Spotlight tracks are selected based on quality, uniqueness, audience fit, and internal scoring. Spotlight rotation includes priority placement and social media boosts.
How long will my track stay in rotation?
Tracks typically remain in rotation for 2–6 weeks depending on performance, rotation level, and show fit. Exceptional tracks may be extended.
Do I keep ownership of my music?
Yes. You retain full ownership of your track. Melanindian Waves only receives permission to broadcast your track during rotation.
Can I update or remove my track?
Yes. Contact support to request removal or submit an updated version. Removal requests are processed within 24 hours.
