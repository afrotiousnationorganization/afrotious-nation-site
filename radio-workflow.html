Melanindian Waves — Radio Workflow

Every track submitted to Melanindian Waves goes through a structured review and placement workflow. This ensures fairness, quality, and consistency across all 7 nightly shows. Below is the complete breakdown of how your track moves through the system.

1. Submission Received
Once your track is uploaded through the Nightscape Radio submission page, it enters our secure review queue. You will receive a confirmation that your submission was successfully received.
2. Initial Review
Our team checks your track for:

• Audio quality
• Proper metadata
• Clean/explicit accuracy
• Genre classification
• Overall suitability for broadcast

Tracks that pass this stage move forward to placement.
3. Show Placement
Your track is placed into the nightly show that best matches its mood, genre, and energy. Placement is based on:

• Vibe
• Tempo
• Emotional tone
• Production style
• Audience fit

You can view the full show list on the Radio Schedule page.
4. Rotation Assignment
Tracks are assigned a rotation level:

• **Standard Rotation** — 1–2 plays per week
• **Enhanced Rotation** — 3–5 plays per week
• **Spotlight Rotation** — Featured placement + social push

Rotation depends on quality, audience fit, and internal scoring.
5. Spotlight Consideration
Exceptional tracks may be selected for Spotlight Rotation. Spotlight tracks receive:

• Priority placement
• Social media highlights
• TikTok Live boosts
• Additional weekly plays

Spotlight is not guaranteed — it is based on performance and quality.
6. TikTok Live Priority
Tracks with strong energy, viral potential, or standout production may be featured during TikTok Live sessions. This gives creators additional exposure and real-time audience engagement.
7. Broadcast & Monitoring
Once placed, your track enters the broadcast cycle. Our system monitors:

• Listener engagement
• Show fit
• Rotation performance

Tracks may be adjusted, boosted, or extended based on performance.
8. Rotation Duration
Tracks typically remain in rotation for 2–6 weeks depending on:

• Audience response
• Show fit
• Rotation level
• Spotlight status

Creators may submit new tracks at any time.
