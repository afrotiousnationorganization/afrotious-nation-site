Melanindian Waves — Radio Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Melanindian Waves — Nightscape Radio (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects information related to radio submissions and visits to our website. By submitting your track or using our site, you agree to this Policy.

2. Information We Collect

  • Submission Information: Artist name, email address, social links, genre, and track file (MP3/WAV).
  • Communication Data: Messages you send us, removal requests, or support inquiries.
  • Usage Data: Basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps, used for security and performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To review and process your radio submission.
  • To contact you about approval, placement, or questions regarding your track.
  • To feature your track on Melanindian Waves radio, playlists, and promotional content with proper credit.
  • To maintain and improve the performance and security of our website and radio services.

4. Content & Broadcast Use

When you submit music, you grant us permission to store your submission details and broadcast your track on Melanindian Waves, including playlists, rotations, and TikTok Live sessions, in line with the Radio Terms. We do not sell your submitted content or personal details to third parties.

5. Cookies & Analytics

Our site may use basic cookies or analytics tools to understand traffic and improve the experience. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though some features may work differently if cookies are disabled.

6. Data Sharing

We may use trusted third-party services for hosting, analytics, email delivery, or file storage. These providers only receive information necessary to perform their services and are expected to protect it. We do not sell your personal information.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect submission data and personal information. However, no method of transmission or storage on the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices & Rights

  • You may request that we update or correct your submission details.
  • You may request removal of your track from rotation.
  • You may ask us to delete your submission data where legally permitted.

9. Children’s Privacy

Melanindian Waves is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information, contact us to request removal.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Effective Date” at the top. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated Policy.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact:
support@afrotiousnation.org
