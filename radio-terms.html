Melanindian Waves — Radio Terms & Conditions

Effective Date: August 2026

1. Overview

These Radio Terms & Conditions govern all music submissions to Melanindian Waves — Nightscape Radio, including one-time submissions, monthly creator pass submissions, and free submissions granted by promo code. By submitting your track, you agree to these terms.

2. Eligibility

  • You must be the legal owner of the music you submit.
  • You must have full rights to distribute and broadcast the track.
  • You must provide accurate artist and contact information.

3. Music Submission Rules

  • All submissions must be MP3 or WAV format.
  • Explicit tracks must be clearly marked as “Explicit.”
  • Clean versions are required for TikTok Live rotation.
  • Only one track is allowed per one-time submission.
  • Monthly subscribers may submit unlimited tracks.

4. Review & Approval

  • All submissions are reviewed within 24–72 hours.
  • Approval is based on quality, genre fit, and broadcast suitability.
  • Melanindian Waves reserves the right to deny any submission.
  • Denied submissions do not receive refunds.

5. Broadcast Rights

By submitting your track, you grant Melanindian Waves a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to:

  • Broadcast your track on Nightscape Radio and its associated shows.
  • Feature your track in playlists, rotations, and promotional content.
  • Play your track during TikTok Live sessions.

You retain full ownership of your music.

6. Placement & Rotation

  • One-time submissions receive one week of rotation.
  • Monthly subscribers receive ongoing rotation while subscribed.
  • Genre placement is determined by the submitted category.
  • Melanindian Waves may adjust placement for programming needs.

7. Creator Spotlight & Features

  • Monthly subscribers are eligible for weekly and monthly spotlights.
  • Spotlight selection is based on quality, engagement, and fit.
  • Spotlight features are not guaranteed.

8. Refund Policy

  • One-time payments are non-refundable once the track is submitted.
  • Monthly subscriptions may be canceled at any time but are not refunded.
