Melanindian Waves Radio — Official Launch Announcement

August 19, 2026

Afrotious Nation is expanding its creative universe with the launch of Melanindian Waves, our official cosmic radio station. Built to amplify creators, storytellers, and sound‑artists across the realm, Melanindian Waves will feature live shows, curated playlists, creator submissions, and cosmic soundscapes that align with the Afrotious Nation vision.

The radio station will operate as a division of AfrotiousNation.org and will introduce a new submission system, creator placement options, and a dedicated Cosmic Radio page. This marks the beginning of our multi‑platform expansion across web, music, handmade creations, and digital experiences.

Stay tuned for the official Cosmic Radio page launch and creator submission guidelines coming soon.
